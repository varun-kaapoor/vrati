How it works

Three steps. Every day.

No options to configure, no menus to navigate. Open. Tap. Close.

01

Add a habit

Just a name. "Read 20 pages." "Drink water." "Workout." That's it. One field.

02

Tap a circle

Once a day, tap the circle next to your habit. Done. The whole interaction is the entire app.

03

See the month

Every checked day gets a circle on the calendar. Empty days stay empty. The density tells the story.

What's in · What's out

Built around what's missing.

Most habit apps drown the user in features. Vrati does the opposite. Every feature was either kept because it serves the one job, or cut because it didn't.

What's in

  • ✓Name-only habits. One field, no categories.
  • ✓One daily reminder per habit, optional.
  • ✓Calendar view with circled days. Density = consistency.
  • ✓Three stats: current streak, this month, all-time.
  • ✓12-month heatmap. Zoom out from the day to the year.
  • ✓Six monochrome color themes — just the accent changes.
  • ✓Cloud sync across iPhone and iPad.
  • ✓Soft sign-in. Sign in with Apple or email, or skip.

What's deliberately out

  • ✗Gamification. No XP, levels, badges, or pets.
  • ✗Categories, folders, or tags.
  • ✗Per-habit colors or icons.
  • ✗Quantitative habits ("8 glasses of water").
  • ✗Multiple times per day.
  • ✗Negative habits ("no smoking").
  • ✗Social. No friends, sharing, or leaderboards.
  • ✗Paywalls or premium tiers. Vrati is free.

Your data stays yours.

We collect your email and the names of your habits. That's it. No browsing data, no contacts, no analytics SDKs that track you across apps. You can delete your account from inside the app anytime.
