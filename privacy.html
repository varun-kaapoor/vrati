Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Vrati is a habit tracking app built by Varun Kapoor. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it. The short version: we collect as little as possible.

What we collect

When you sign in with Apple or with email and password, we store:

  • Your email address — used to identify your account and send occasional product updates (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Your habits and check-ins — the names of the habits you create, the dates you marked them complete, and any reminder times you set.
  • A unique account ID assigned by Firebase Authentication.

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, browsing history, or any other personal data. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs that track you across apps.

Why we collect it

  • To sync your habits across your devices (e.g. iPhone and iPad).
  • To restore your data if you reinstall the app.
  • To occasionally email you product updates or tips on building habits. You can opt out at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email.

Where it's stored

Your data lives in Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore), located on Google Cloud servers in the United States. Firebase is operated by Google LLC and is governed by their privacy policy. Vrati does not share your data with any other third party.

Skipping login

If you tap "Skip for now" on the welcome screen, your habits stay only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. You can sign in later from Settings to enable cloud sync — at that point your existing local habits will be uploaded to your account.

Your rights

  • Access: You can see all the data we have about you inside the app.
  • Deletion: Deleting your account removes all your habits, check-ins, and email from our servers within 30 days. Email varun37kapoor@icloud.com to request account deletion.
  • Correction: You can edit or delete habits at any time from inside the app.
  • Export: Email us and we'll send you a copy of your data within 30 days.

Children

Vrati is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and notify you in-app on next launch. Continued use after a change means you accept the new policy.

Contact

Questions? Email varun37kapoor@icloud.com.
